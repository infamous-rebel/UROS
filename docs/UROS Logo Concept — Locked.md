
# UROS Logo Concept — Locked

## Concept Name

**Anamorphic Breathing Bloom**

## Visual Idea

A soft, round, flower-like symbol formed by layered spirals and fine bud-like strokes.

It feels:

-   Organic, but structured
-   Calm, floating
-   Like a bloom breathing in slow wind
-   Unknown — not a literal flower, more like a living process
-   Soothing, meditative

## Structure

-   Center: a small soft core — not a solid dot, maybe a faint glow
-   Around it: 3–4 layered spiral arms
-   The arms overlap gently, not symmetrical and rigid
-   Fine hair-like filaments at the edges of each spiral — like buds catching air
-   The whole form sits inside a soft circular boundary, but the boundary is suggested, not drawn

## Motion Behavior

-   The spirals rotate very slowly, like petals opening and closing
-   The hair-like buds sway gently, as if moved by wind
-   The whole form pulses subtly — breathing, not spinning
-   No sharp loops, no mechanical spin
-   At rest, it looks like a still bloom
-   During loading, it gently exhales and shifts

## Single Color Usage

-   **Deep Teal #0F766E** for product and favicon
-   **Terracotta #E2725B** for marketing and human-centered moments
-   On dark backgrounds, use warm off-white `#FAF9F5`

## Favicon Behavior

At 16px:

-   Reads as a soft round bloom with a visible center and fine outer texture
-   No text, no hard edges
-   The gap between layers creates the open feel

## Loading Symbol Behavior

-   The bloom slowly rotates or morphs
-   Layers expand and contract slightly
-   The fine buds sway
-   Feels like watching a calm living organism

## Summary

UROS logo = anamorphic breathing bloom

-   Layered spirals
-   Hairy bud filaments
-   Floating, waving motion
-   Single color
-   Works as favicon and loading symbol
-   Calm, organic, structured flow
